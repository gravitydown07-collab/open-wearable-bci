# Scripture Index

**Anchored by Rob Roy — John 3:16 is the root. Everything flows from it.**

## The Eight Pillars

1. **Body as Temple** — 1 Corinthians 6:19-20. Your body is the temple of the Holy Ghost. Any device touching it must honor that.
2. **God Alone Knows the Heart** — 1 Samuel 16:7. Man looks on the outward appearance, but the Lord looks on the heart. No brain-based scoring, profiling, or judgment of a person's worth.
3. **Renewal of Mind Is His Work** — Romans 12:2. Be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind. Technology assists; it does not replace the Spirit's work.
4. **The Least of These** — Matthew 25:40. Inasmuch as ye have done it unto one of the least of these my brethren, ye have done it unto me. Design for the vulnerable first.
5. **Speak Up for Those Who Can't** — Proverbs 31:8. Open thy mouth for the dumb in the cause of all such as are appointed to destruction. Assistive tech exists to give voice back.
6. **No Parapet, No Fall** — Deuteronomy 22:8. When thou buildest a new house, then thou shalt make a battlement for thy roof. Safety built in from day one, not patched later.
7. **The Sick Need a Physician** — Matthew 9:12. They that be whole need not a physician, but they that are sick. Health claims get a clinician, not a guess.
8. **The Sabbath Rest** — Exodus 20:8-10. Six days shalt thou labour... but the seventh is the sabbath. Even the builders rest. Extended breaks are not laziness — they are obedience.

## Scripture in Practice

Before any change that affects the wearer gets merged, answer four questions:

1. Does this honor the body as a temple?
2. Does this respect that God alone knows the heart?
3. Does this serve the least of these?
4. Does this keep the wearer free to walk away?

If a verse does not actually settle a question, write **"no Scripture anchor"** instead of forcing one. That is intellectual honesty dressed in humility. Deuteronomy 22:8 and Proverbs 31:8 are fair extensions of their original settings, not direct tech statements — nobody can accuse this project of proof-texting.

## Translations

Every quoted verse appears in each language's own published Bible translation with named source. John 3:16 leads in every tongue. Scripture stays KJV in English files; surrounding text translates.
