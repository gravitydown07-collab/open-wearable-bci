# Contributing Guide

**Led by Rob Roy — John 3:16 is the anchor.**

## Who Can Contribute

You don't have to share the faith to contribute. You have to share the aim: prosperity for everybody, not just a few. Eliminate fake scarcity. Make work optional through collaboration between humans and the tools God gave us.

## How to Contribute

1. Fork the repo.
2. Work in your own branch.
3. Keep changes small and reviewable.
4. Before any change that affects the wearer, answer the four Scripture-in-Practice questions in `/scripture/SCRIPTURE_INDEX.md`.
5. Open a pull request. Tag it with the lane it belongs to: tech, safety, ethics, care, health, scripture, or docs.
6. A lane owner reviews before merge.

## License

Code and hardware: CERN Open Hardware License. Written docs: CC BY 4.0. Fork it, build it, sell it, modify it — no permission needed, no royalties owed. That's the whole point.

## What We Don't Merge

- Anything that adds paywalls.
- Anything that adds stimulation without a clinician path.
- Anything that reads neural data without explicit consent.
- Anything that scores or profiles a person's brain for third-party benefit.
- Anything that removes the physical halt button or the off switch.
