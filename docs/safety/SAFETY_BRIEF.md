# Safety Brief — Open Wearable BCI

**Primary verse: Deuteronomy 22:8** — When thou buildest a new house, then thou shalt make a battlement for thy roof, that thou bring not blood upon thine house, if any man fall from thence.

**Led by Rob Roy**

## Scope

Read-only wearables. No stimulation in v1. Stimulation requires a clinician and a hard stop the wearer controls, and stays on paper for clinical partners only.

## Top Safeguards

1. **Clinician involved in any health use** — no self-diagnosis from raw EEG.
2. **No flashing screens** — flicker off by default, seizure warning present. Twenty-nine out of thirty consumer EEG companies had unrestricted access to neural data; that pattern stops here.
3. **Intentional commands, not guessed moods** — the device responds to what you choose to send, not what it infers you feel.
4. **Data stays on device** — one simple stop-and-delete. Nothing uploaded unless the wearer explicitly opts in.
5. **One physical halt button** — big, obvious, always reachable. Software kill switches are not enough.

## Honest Limits

- Best EEG spellers hit about twelve words per minute versus thirty-two to sixty-two for implants.
- Consumer EEG evidence is shaky; treat it as assistive, not medical.
- No legal review completed yet.
- No input yet from disabled users who might need this most — that gap is on the roadmap, not an afterthought.

## Hard Lines

- No covert reading of bystanders.
- No brain-based scoring for jobs, credit, or church standing.
- No stimulation that messes with belief or worship.
- The wearer can always take it off without losing anything.
